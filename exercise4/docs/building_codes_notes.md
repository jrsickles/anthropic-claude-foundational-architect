## Energy Code

Our region (assume: North Carolina) is currently enforcing the 2018 IECC
with state amendments. There has been internal discussion about a possible
future move toward the 2021 or 2024 IECC, but as of the last internal
review no adoption timeline has been confirmed locally.

## Wildfire / Resilience

No wildland-urban interface (WUI) code has been adopted in our
jurisdiction. Flood provisions follow the locally adopted floodplain
ordinance, last updated several years ago; it has not been revisited since
the current ASCE flood standard was released.

## Accessibility / Egress

Multifamily projects in our jurisdiction are still required to provide two
exit stairs regardless of building height or unit count. No single-stair
reform proposal has been adopted locally.
