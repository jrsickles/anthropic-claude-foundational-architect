# Internal Pasture Management Notes

Document type: internal operations reference
Last reviewed: 2025-03-10

## Rotational Grazing Schedule

Paddocks are rotated on a 21-day rest cycle during the spring/summer growing
season (April-September), shortened to a 35-day rest cycle in the fall as
grass growth slows. Cattle are moved between 6 paddocks of roughly equal
size. This schedule has been in place, unchanged, since the 2023 season.

## Soil Testing

Soil pH and nutrient testing is performed once per year, in early spring,
across all 6 paddocks. As of the last test (2025-03-10), average soil pH
across paddocks was 6.1, within the target range of 6.0-6.8 for the grass
mix currently seeded. No lime application has been needed since 2024.

## Water Infrastructure

Each paddock has one gravity-fed water trough. No automated or remote water
monitoring is in place. Troughs are checked manually during daily herd
checks.

## Fencing

All paddock boundaries use traditional permanent electric fencing. No
virtual or GPS-based fencing technology has been evaluated or piloted as of
this document's last review date.

## Equipment

A single tractor-mounted mower is used for clipping ungrazed forage
approximately every 4-6 weeks during the growing season. No drone or
remote-sensing equipment is currently in use for pasture monitoring.

## Open Question

Given how long the current rotation schedule and fencing setup have been in
place unchanged, are there newer approaches (e.g., higher-density rotational
grazing, virtual fencing) that would be worth piloting?
